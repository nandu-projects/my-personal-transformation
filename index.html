Daily 3-Min Posture Stretches

"These habits support posture, fitness and general health. Adult height cannot be guaranteed to increase."
1. Wall Angels (45 sec) Stand with back, head, and elbows flat against wall. Slide arms up and down keeping shoulder blades tight.
2. Chin Tucks (30 sec) Look forward, gently draw chin straight backward (like making a double chin). Hold 3s, release. Corrects forward neck posture from study/screens.
3. Doorway Chest Stretch (45 sec) Place forearms on door frame, step one foot forward and feel chest expand. Opens rounded shoulders.
4. Cat-Cow Spine Mobility (60 sec) On hands and knees, alternate gentle arching and rounding of spine to decompress vertebrae.
