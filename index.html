🧘 3-Minute Posture Routine

"Helps posture, mobility and general fitness. It does not guarantee an increase in adult height."
1. Wall Angels (45 sec) Stand with back, head, and elbows flat against wall. Slide arms up and down keeping shoulder blades engaged.
2. Chin Tucks (30 sec) Look forward, gently draw chin straight backward (like making a double chin). Hold 3s, release. Corrects forward neck posture from study and screens.
3. Doorway Chest Stretch (45 sec) Place forearms on door frame, step one foot forward and feel chest open comfortably.
4. Cat-Cow Spine Flow (60 sec) On hands and knees, alternate gentle arching and rounding of spine to decompress vertebrae.
